Company Benefits and Workplace Policies 2026

1. Remote Work and Home Office Allowance
All full-time employees are eligible for a one-time remote setup stipend of $1,500 upon hiring. In addition, an ongoing monthly wellness and internet reimbursement of $120 is provided through the Expensify portal.

2. Annual Learning & Development Budget
Each employee is allocated an annual $2,500 L&D budget for conferences, technical certifications, AI coursework, and relevant books. Prior manager approval is required for expenses exceeding $500.

3. Global Innovation Hackathon
Our company hackathon ("HackNova") takes place twice a year (June and November). Engineering sprints are paused for 72 hours, and top projects receive production sponsorship and cash prizes totaling $25,000.

4. Paid Time Off (PTO)
We offer flexible open PTO for salaried employees with a recommended minimum of 20 days off annually. Parental leave provides 16 weeks of fully paid leave for all new parents.
